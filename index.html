Our Story

Seven Winds Travel & Tours began as the personal project of its Director — a former traveler who journeyed across all seven nations of Teyvat firsthand: from the windswept hills of Mondstadt and the harbor markets of Liyue, to the shrine islands of Inazuma, the rainforest city of Sumeru, the canals of Fontaine, the volcanic plains of Natlan, and the frozen reaches of Snezhnaya.

Word of that firsthand knowledge spread among friends and fellow travelers. Casual trip-planning favors, shared through chat groups and social media, slowly grew into a small, personal travel consultancy.

Today, travelers regularly ask about all seven nations at once, and every nation has its own culture, pricing, itinerary length, and seasonal rhythm. This website, our Teyvat Travel Services storefront, is our first official home: one place to explore every tour package, learn who is guiding you, and find answers before you ever have to ask.

Meet Our Director

Having traveled across all seven nations of Teyvat, our founder experienced firsthand the landscapes, cultures, and stories that make each region unique. Those journeys inspired the creation of Seven Winds Travel & Tours — an agency built to help others discover the same sense of wonder.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about pricing, groups, and how to start. Can’t find yours? Go to the inquiry form.

  • How do I book a tour across Teyvat?

    This website is a demo. The forms check your details in the browser, but do not send or save them. Online booking and payment are not available.

  • What’s included in the base price?

    The “from” price on each nation page is per person and covers the core package: accommodation for the nights listed, guided visits to the three featured stops, local transfers between them, and itinerary planning by the Director.

  • What’s not included?

    Not included in the base price:

    • Travel to your starting point
    • Meals and personal expenses not listed in the itinerary
    • Optional add-ons and upgrades
    • Pass or permit fees, where required
  • How many people can join a tour?

    Prices are shown per person. The demo form accepts groups of 1 to 20 travelers, but it does not send a request or confirm availability.

  • Are travel passes or permits required for restricted nations like Inazuma?

    This demo does not check real travel requirements. Check the latest official guidance before planning a real trip.

  • Can I customize a multi-nation tour itinerary?

    The demo shows one nation per package. It does not create custom itineraries or calculate combined trip prices.

  • What safety measures are provided during high-risk region tours?

    This demo does not arrange tours, guides, or safety support.

  • Will my inquiry get a reply?

    No. The demo form does not send or save your details, so nobody receives the inquiry.
