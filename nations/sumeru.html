Good to know

Plan ahead for your Sumeru journey.

  • Best season

    The rainforest is humid year-round; drier months are easier.

  • Getting around

    Guided city walks and caravan-road transfers.

  • Bring

    Valid ID and your confirmation details.

  • What to wear

    Light, breathable clothing and insect protection.
