Good to know

Plan ahead for your Inazuma journey.

  • Best season

    Spring brings sakura; other seasons see frequent storms.

  • Getting around

    Island-to-island crossings with guided transfers.

  • Bring

    Valid ID and the Inazuma travel pass, where required.

  • What to wear

    Rain-ready layers; modest attire for shrine visits.
